About

I have spent the last 3.5 years at Apprication owning core modules of QuickVee, a POS and order management product used by retail stores in the US. My work sits close to the hardware: card terminals, receipt printers and barcode scanners, so reliability matters more than anything else.

I write Kotlin with MVVM, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines and Flow, and I care about the parts people do not see: token refresh that never logs a cashier out, lists that page smoothly through thousands of products, and crash rates that stay low.
